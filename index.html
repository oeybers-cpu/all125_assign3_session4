ALL 124 Assignment 3: Team Argumentation Presentation Planning

Complete these sections in sequence to ensure your team is fully prepared for the presentation. All notes will be saved locally in your browser.

Define Thesis Statements

Guiding questions:

  • What is our team's clear, debatable position on the chosen issue?
  • How does our thesis statement address the 'why' and 'so what' of our argument?
  • What specific elements must our thesis include (position, scope, rationale)?
  • How will this thesis guide our evidence selection and structure?

Relevant to Evaluation Criteria:

  • Clarity and strength of argument (8 marks)
  • Use of evidence from required texts (6 marks)

Final Argumentative Theme & Topic

Guiding questions:

  • What specific theme from Assignment 2 have we selected? (Unemployment, Corruption, Entrepreneurship, etc.)
  • What precise debatable issue within this theme will we address?
  • Why is this issue particularly relevant to current societal or academic discussions?
  • How does our chosen topic meet the assignment requirements for a debatable issue?

Relevant to Evaluation Criteria:

  • Clarity and strength of argument (8 marks)
  • Use of evidence from required texts (6 marks)

Identify Key Data

Guiding questions:

  • What are the two key articles we will use as primary evidence? (Include full titles and authors)
  • What specific data, statistics, or arguments from each article support our thesis?
  • How will we cite these sources using Harvard style in our presentation?
  • How do these sources help us address potential counterarguments?
  • How will we incorporate one visual aid (graph, chart, or diagram) to support our argument?

Relevant to Evaluation Criteria:

  • Use of evidence from required texts (6 marks)
  • Visual aid effectiveness (5 marks)
  • Acknowledgement and rebuttal of counterarguments (5 marks)

Finalise Roles & Format

Guiding questions:

  • What specific roles will each team member take? (Researcher, Presenter, Visual Designer, etc.)
  • Will our presentation be face-to-face or digital? What factors influenced this decision?
  • How will we ensure equal contribution from all team members throughout the process?
  • What specific responsibilities does each role have for the presentation structure?
  • How will we manage the 8-10 minute time limit for our presentation?

Relevant to Evaluation Criteria:

  • Team coordination and contribution (4 marks)
  • Clarity and strength of argument (8 marks)
  • Strong, concise conclusion (2 marks)

Contact Details & Meeting Plan

Guiding questions:

  • What are each team member's contact details (email, phone, preferred communication method)?
  • When and where will our next team meeting take place? (Include virtual meeting link if applicable)
  • What specific tasks must be completed before our next meeting?
  • How will we maintain communication between meetings? (e.g., WhatsApp, email, Teams)
  • What is our deadline for completing all presentation elements?

Relevant to Evaluation Criteria:

  • Team coordination and contribution (4 marks)
  • Strong, concise conclusion (2 marks)

Important Reminders

Key reminders for your presentation submission:

  • 1) Presentation format: Presentations may be face-to-face or digital. Choose the format that best suits your team's needs and resources.
  • 2) Digital narration: Digital presentations must include either spoken narration (recorded voice) or digital narration (e.g., AI voice generation). Ensure audio quality is clear and professional.
  • 3) Submission responsibility: Only the team leader submits on behalf of the team in ClickUP. All student names and student numbers must be included in the submission document or description.
  • 4) Individual submissions: Individual submissions cannot receive 75% or higher. Group work must be submitted as a team for full marks.
  • 5) Deadline: All submissions must be made by 31 October at 23:59 SAST. Late submissions will be penalized according to university policy.
  • 6) Turnitin check: Your submission will undergo Turnitin plagiarism checking. Ensure all sources are properly cited using Harvard style.
  • 7) Technical check: Test your presentation file or link before submission to ensure it opens correctly and plays without errors.

Additional tips for success:

  • Practice your presentation as a team to ensure smooth transitions and timing
  • Confirm all team members understand their roles and responsibilities
  • Double-check all contact information for team members
  • Save multiple versions of your presentation files (e.g., .pptx, .pdf, .key)
  • Have backup plans for technical issues (e.g., alternative device, cloud storage access)
